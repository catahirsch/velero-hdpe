3. VERDICT  (governing case: light ship, cockpit flooded, tanks empty)
==============================================================================
  Bare hull (baseline):   AVS 110 deg, stable inverted,
                          0.342 m.rad = 2516 J holding it turtle.
  With sealed rig+float:  GZ stays positive past 110 deg --
                          the boat recovers unaided from any heel up to 155.8 deg.
                          Residual inverted pocket: 155.8-180 deg, 0.0411 m.rad = 302 J
                          (8x shallower than the baseline's).
  Energy barrier guarding that pocket (area 110-156 deg): 0.358 m.rad = 2633 J --
  to turtle, a seaway must push the boat through ALL of it; to release,
  it only has to nudge the boat 24.2 deg. In any water rough
  enough to capsize this boat, the pocket is not a resting state.

  Claim for the spec sheet / placard: AUTO-ADRIZANTE con quilla abajo y
  trabada y tanques vacios; el flotador de tope hace el 180 inalcanzable
  y el barco vuelve solo desde cualquier escora hasta 156 grados.

4. FLOAT SIZING  (why 60 L: sweep at 31 stations, 5 deg)
==============================================================================
   float (L)  rec.limit  neg area  GZ>0 to 180
           0      123.4    0.2211        False
          20      137.6    0.1279        False
          40      148.1    0.0726        False
          60      155.9    0.0396        False
          80      162.0    0.0208        False
  Below ~40 L the float cannot carry the immersed rig's weight with
  margin (rig 32 kg + float, buoyancy needed > ~35 kg); 60 L gives a
  1.7x margin and costs 2.5 kg. Beyond 80 L the recovery limit barely
  moves -- the residual pocket is pinched against 180 by symmetry, not
  by float size.
