# Cost analysis — building in Chile

Generated by `calc/costs.py` (full line-item tables in `out/costs.txt`). Every
unit price is a **low/mid/high planning band**, tagged `[QUOTE]` where a real
Chilean quote is needed, `[MKT]` where international pricing is stable, `[EST]`
for engineering estimates. **These are 2026 planning numbers, not offers** —
the script exists so the client's real quotes replace the bands and the totals
recompute consistently.

Assumptions: 950 CLP/USD · IVA 19% included in every figure · imports +15%
freight, 0% duty (Chile's FTAs with EU/US/China cover essentially all the
marine gear).

## 2. What the numbers actually say

**The hull is not where the money is.** Welded PE shell material is ~USD 3,100
mid — about 5% of the boat. The cost lives in the systems: the **electric
drivetrain (motor + battery ≈ USD 9,900 mid, 24% of materials)** is the single
largest block, then rig + sails (≈ 11,600), then hardware and systems. This is
the cost-side confirmation of what the weight budget already showed: the
electric auxiliary, not the HDPE, is the expensive decision.

**At a series of 20, rotomoulding ≈ welded sheet in cost per boat** (63.6 vs
64.4 k mid): the tooling amortisation (~5,400/boat) is almost exactly cancelled
by the labour saving (~7,500/boat). Rotomoulding wins on consistency, finish,
the true double-skin structure (lighter shell → the full 213 kg keel), and
per-unit cost keeps falling beyond 20 units. **For one boat it is not a
contest: welded sheet, USD ~64 k mid, vs carrying a 60–130 k tool on a single
hull.**

**The realistic programme is Route A first anyway** — the prototype validates
the roll test, the water-ballast conversation with the Capitanía, and the
operating rules, before anyone cuts a tool.

## 3. Chile-specific notes

- **Sheet suppliers to quote** (all active, from the supplier search):
  [Plastigen](https://plastigen.cl/productos/polimeros-de-ingenieria/comprar-polietileno/),
  [Polymerland](https://www.polymerland.cl/contenido.php?id_articulo=238),
  [Plastisan](https://plastisan.cl/211-placa-hdpe),
  [Siplas](https://www.siplas.cl/placashdpe),
  [Servicios Plastisur (Puerto Montt)](http://www.serviciosplastisur.cl/productos.php?prod=3) —
  the last one matters if the build is in the south. None publish per-kg
  prices; the 3.5–6.0 USD/kg band is the expected quote range for PE500
  cut-to-size.
- **Rotomoulding capability is the open question for Route B.** Chilean
  rotomoulders routinely make large water tanks, but a 6.5 m boat half needs
  ≥4 m of machine swing per piece — confirm capability (or price importing
  moulded shells from Brazil/Argentina) before taking Route B seriously.
- **PE welding labour**: HDPE extrusion welding is an established trade in
  Chile via the aquaculture industry (salmon-farm pontoons and pipework, HDPE
  boats already exist in Puerto Montt/Chiloé) — this is genuinely favourable
  for Route A, both for rates and for finding welders certified to DVS-style
  practice.
- **Lead**: cast locally (fishing-weight and battery foundries); LME basis
  ~2.1 USD/kg plus casting.
- **Electric outboard pricing** is US-list based
  ([ePropulsion Navy series](https://www.epropulsion.com/products/electric-outboards/navy-evo);
  the [Practical Sailor buyer's guide](https://www.practical-sailor.com/systems-propulsion/electrical/buyers-guide-14-electric-outboard-options-from-2-5-to-9-9-horsepower/)
  is a good option survey) — Chilean dealer margins can add 10–25% over the
  modelled freight; a direct-import quote is worth it.

## 4. Sensitivity (mid-case, Route A)

| Driver | Swing | Effect on total |
|---|---|---|
| Shop labour rate 14→25 USD/h | +11 USD/h | +USD 10,500 |
| Electric drive → best-price import | −20% | −USD 2,000 |
| CLP devalues 950→1100 | −14% CLP | CLP total +16 M (USD total ~flat; imported share is USD-linked) |
| PE500 quote 4.5→3.5 USD/kg | −22% | −USD 570 (small — see §2) |
| Drop battery to 1.5 kWh | −USD 900 | and returns ~16 kg to the keel |

Labour rate dominates. Getting a fixed-price shell quote from an aquaculture
HDPE fabricator is the single most valuable quote in the whole exercise.

## 5. How to use this

1. Send `docs/04-build-spec.md` §2–3 + the offsets to two PE fabricators
   (one in the Santiago area, one in Puerto Montt) for a shell quote.
2. Get the drivetrain quoted both from a Chilean dealer and as direct import.
3. Replace the `[QUOTE]` bands in `calc/costs.py` with the numbers, re-run
   `python3 -m calc.costs`, and the totals update with tax and freight applied
   consistently.
