==============================================================================
SELF-RIGHTING TRADE STUDY
==============================================================================
Requirement: GZ > 0 at every angle up to 180 deg, cockpit flooded,
light ship (crew in the water). Displacement floats free in these
sweeps -- ballast is added on top of the fixed weight.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ballast    disp   ratio   GZmax     AVS  self-right
       213     751   28.4%   0.667   112.2       False
       300     838   35.8%   0.730   119.6       False
       400     938   42.7%   0.791   126.3       False
       500    1038   48.2%   0.839   131.7       False
       600    1138   52.7%   0.864   136.1       False
       800    1338   59.8%   0.898   143.4       False
      1000    1538   65.0%   0.934   148.9       False

  Ballast alone does not get there at this beam, at any weight that

==============================================================================
READING
==============================================================================
  True 180-degree self-righting is a beam problem before it is a
  ballast problem. The same wide, shallow hull that carries a 6-seat
  cockpit and 22 m2 of sail is stable upside down, and no amount of
  lead that a 6.5 m hull can float changes that at 2.35 m beam.

  The requirement and the brief are mutually exclusive as written.
  Something has to give -- see docs/03-self-righting.md.
