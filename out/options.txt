------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTION A -- masthead buoyancy (prevents inversion instead of curing it)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Baseline as designed: AVS 111 deg, inverted-stable, energy holding it there 0.33 m.rad
  A masthead float does not appear on a GZ curve -- it works by making
  180 degrees unreachable, so the boat stops at roughly the AVS angle
  (111 deg) and the crew right it from there with the floor straps
  already in the brief (notes line 46).

  Sizing: to hold the rig up at ~110 deg the float must displace more
  than the rig's submerged weight plus a margin. For a 32 kg alloy rig
  a 45-60 litre masthead float is the usual answer: about 2.5 kg.
  Cost: 2.5 kg and some windage aloft. Nothing else in the brief moves.
